## Reflection

### 1. Did the estimations reflect the actual effort?

The estimates generally reflected the relative effort and complexity of
the User Stories. Smaller activities were estimated at 3 points, core
workflow stories at 5 points, and the more complex route optimization,
tracking, and security work at 8 points.

### 2. Was the backlog well-prioritized?

Yes. The backlog prioritized core courier-delivery functionality and
operationally important security features. Multi-stop route optimization
was assigned medium priority because it improves efficiency after the
core delivery flow is established.

### 3. How did the simulated sprint align with the plan?

The simulation followed the planned Agile flow of To Do → In Progress →
Done. Sprint 1 contained 21 points and Sprint 2 contained 29 points,
covering all 9 planned User Stories.

### 4. What insights did the Burndown Chart give about team capacity?

The Burndown Charts showed how story points decreased as work was
completed. Sprint 1 reached zero remaining points, while Sprint 2
progressed from 29 points to zero. The charts provided a clear view of
completion progress and helped evaluate the team's capacity and timing
during the simulated sprints.
